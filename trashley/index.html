Trashley

Coming soon to Google Play

Your camera roll, sorted one month at a time. Swipe left to bin, right to keep, then watch the space come back.

How it works

  • Pick a month. Trashley shows its photos one by one, oldest first.
  • Swipe left to bin, right to keep. Changed your mind? Undo.
  • Check what you binned, rescue anything you want to keep, then bin the rest in one go.
  • Binned photos go to your phone's own bin, so you can still restore them for about 30 days.

Private by design

Trashley works entirely on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded, and the app has no internet access at all. There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking. The details are in the privacy policy.

Help

Something not working? Use Settings → Something wrong? in the app, or email support@twothirds.studio.
