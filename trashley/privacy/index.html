Trashley privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

The short version

  • Trashley works entirely on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded or shared.
  • The app has no internet access, so it can't send anything to us or anyone else.
  • No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking.
  • If you email us, we use your email only to help you.

Who we are

Trashley is made by TwoThirds Studio ("we", "us"), the trading name of Arandeep Atwal, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. For anything about privacy, email privacy@twothirds.studio.

Your photos stay on your phone

Trashley needs access to your photos so you can decide which to keep and which to bin. It reads them only on your device. Your photos, what's in them, their file names and their locations are never uploaded, copied off your phone or shared with anyone.

If you give Trashley access to only some of your photos, it works with just those.

Binning photos

When you bin photos, Trashley asks Android to move them to your phone's own bin. Android shows a confirmation for each batch, and nothing moves unless you agree. Binned photos usually stay in your phone's bin for about 30 days, so you can restore them. Trashley never permanently deletes anything itself.

What Trashley stores on your phone

  • A record of which photos you've kept or binned, so it can show your progress and remember where you got to.
  • Your settings, such as your light or dark theme choice.

This stays on your phone. It isn't sent to us and isn't included in your phone's backups. To delete it, clear Trashley's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall the app.

We don't collect any data

Trashley doesn't have internet permission, so it can't connect to the internet at all. It has no analytics, no crash reporting service, no ads, no advertising ID and no account to sign in to. We don't receive anything about you or how you use the app.

If we ever add something like optional, anonymous usage stats, it will be off unless you turn it on, and we'll update this policy before we do.

Sharing your month

When you finish a month, you can tap Flex on your friends. Trashley makes an image of that month's numbers (such as how many photos you binned and how much space you freed up) and opens Android's share menu. The image contains only those numbers, never your photos. You choose where it goes, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

Emailing us

If you tap Something wrong? in Settings, Trashley opens your email app with a message to support@twothirds.studio. It fills in your app version and phone model, which you can see and change before sending. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to fix problems, and never for marketing. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering your questions and fixing the app.

We keep a conversation while we're helping you, and for a while afterwards in case the same problem comes back. Once a year we delete conversations we no longer need. Our email provider stores support emails for us in UK data centres. We don't share them with anyone else.

Keeping your information safe

Trashley's record of your decisions is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read, and it's left out of backups so it never leaves your phone. Our email account is protected by two-step sign-in, and our email provider encrypts email in transit whenever the other side supports it (almost all email services do).

Google Play

Google Play handles downloading and updating Trashley, under Google's privacy policy. Google gives us summary statistics, such as install counts. If you've chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google, it may also give us crash reports, which don't identify you.

Children

Trashley isn't aimed at children under 13.

Your rights

Because the app sends us nothing, we don't hold any information about you from using Trashley. If you've emailed us, you have the right to ask for a copy of what we hold, to have it corrected or deleted, or to ask us to limit how we use it. Email privacy@twothirds.studio and we'll reply within a month.

You also have the right to object to us using your information on the basis of our legitimate interests. If you do, we'll stop, unless we have a compelling reason to continue.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what the app does with your information, we'll say so in the app's release notes too.
